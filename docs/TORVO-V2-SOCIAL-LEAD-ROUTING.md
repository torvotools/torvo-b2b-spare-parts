# TORVO V2 SOCIAL LEAD ROUTING

## PURPOSE
Route customers/dealers arriving from social profiles into the same secure TORVO requirement/referral CRM instead of creating a disconnected social database.

## APPROVED ENTRY POINTS
- WEBSITE PRODUCT DISCOVERY
- SEND REQUIREMENT
- WHATSAPP BUSINESS/API
- PUBLIC BUSINESS EMAIL
- BECOME A DEALER / DEALER REGISTRATION

## SOURCE ATTRIBUTION
Where technically available, capture a non-sensitive lead source such as WEBSITE, FACEBOOK, INSTAGRAM, YOUTUBE, WHATSAPP, EMAIL or OTHER. Never fabricate source attribution when it is unknown.

## CUSTOMER FLOW
SOCIAL -> TORVO WEBSITE/WHATSAPP -> PRODUCT OR REQUIREMENT -> MINIMUM CUSTOMER DETAILS -> REFERRAL CRM -> ELIGIBLE DEALER/TORVO FOLLOW-UP.

## DEALER FLOW
SOCIAL -> BECOME A DEALER -> REGISTRATION -> TORVO VERIFICATION -> APPROVAL -> APP ACCESS.

## PRIVACY
- Keep enquiry/referral consent separate from promotional marketing consent.
- Do not expose private Admin email or Dealer-private commercial information.
- Do not create fake WhatsApp sent/delivered states.
- Customer/referral data remains role-authorized and non-enumerable publicly.
